About Us

Sign Board Design, Fabrication & Installation.

Strategic signage partners for lasting brand impact.

Arkit unites consultants, designers, engineers, and fabricators to shape signage programs that stay true to each client's brand voice and business goals.

From zoning research and permitting to installation and ongoing refreshes, our team delivers dependable project leadership and measurable visibility results.

  • Dedicated project managers for every rollout and retrofit.
  • In‑house CNC, laser, and powder‑coat production for precise finishes.
  • Certified installers with nationwide coverage and safety credentials.
  • Proactive maintenance and compliance programs after launch.
